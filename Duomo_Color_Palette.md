# Lindsey Dortch — Personal Brand Color Palette
### Inspired by the Cattedrale di Santa Maria del Fiore, Florence, Italy

---

## The Palette

### Background
| Name | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Warm Charcoal | `#1C1917` | Primary background, pulled from the shadow tones of the Duomo stone |

### Primary Accent — Forest Green
| Name | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Deep Green | `#2D5A3D` | Primary accent, inspired by the deep green marble inlay |
| Vibrant Green | `#3D7A54` | Use when you need slightly more vibrancy |

### Secondary Accent — Rose Marble
| Name | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Blush Rose | `#C9A99A` | Secondary accent, inspired by the warm blush pink marble panels |
| Deep Rose | `#B8897A` | Use when you need a deeper, richer tone |

### Tertiary Accent — Florentine Gold
| Name | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Warm Gold | `#C9A84C` | Special moments, highlights, and details |
| Bright Gold | `#D4A832` | Use when you need more punch |

### Text
| Name | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Marble White | `#F5F0EB` | All body text and headings, warm and elevated |

---

## Recommended Usage for the Deck

| Element | Color | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Background | Warm Charcoal | `#1C1917` |
| Primary Accent | Deep Green | `#2D5A3D` |
| Secondary Accent | Blush Rose | `#C9A99A` |
| Special Highlights | Warm Gold | `#C9A84C` |
| All Text | Marble White | `#F5F0EB` |

---

## The Inspiration

> The Cattedrale di Santa Maria del Fiore, Florence, Italy.
> The deep green marble inlay, warm rose marble panels, golden gilded details, and creamy stone facade of the Duomo inform every color in this palette.
